Keyboard navigation for ChatGPT

Fly through ChatGPT without touching the mouse

Kity is a lightweight browser extension that lets you control ChatGPT with smart keyboard shortcuts: jump between your messages, scroll smoothly, copy text instantly – all without hunting for the mouse.

Try Kity for free →

What Kity helps you do:

  • Toggle pane focus (Ctrl + Left/Right) – jump between chat and sidebar.
  • Smooth scrolling (Ctrl + Up/Down) – move quickly through long chats.
  • Smart copy (Ctrl + C) – copy text under focus instantly.
  • Jump between your messages (Ctrl + Shift + Up/Down) – skip AI responses.
  • Jump to first/last message (Ctrl + A / Z) – go straight to the top or bottom.
  • Open folders/chats (Ctrl + Enter) – control the left sidebar with keys.
